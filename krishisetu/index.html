✨ Platform Features

Everything a Farmer Needs

From booking a procurement slot to receiving payment — KrishiSetu digitizes the entire agricultural procurement journey.

Smart Slot Booking

Book your procurement slot in advance. Receive a digital token with QR code. No more waiting in long queues at the mandi.

Live Queue Tracking

Track your position in real-time. Get notified when your turn is approaching. See estimated wait times and queue health.

AI Market Advisory

Get AI-powered insights on the best time and place to sell. Compare nearby mandis with transport-adjusted net value economics.

Digital Payments (DBT)

Track Direct Benefit Transfer payments in real-time. Transparent procurement records with quality grading and MSP rates.
